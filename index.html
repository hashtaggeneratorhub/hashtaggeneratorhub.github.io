Why hashtags still matter

Hashtags are how platforms sort content. A good tag puts your post in front of people who are already interested in that topic, even if they do not follow you yet. The trick is relevance: a tag should describe what the post is actually about, not just what is popular today.

Use fewer, better tags. Five relevant tags usually beat thirty random ones. Check the guides below to learn research, tracking, and platform-specific rules.

Quick rules

Keep tags relevant to the post. Avoid banned or broken tags. Do not repeat the exact same set on every post. Test new tags in small batches and keep the ones that earn reach.
