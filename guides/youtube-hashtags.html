YouTube Hashtags: How They Work in Titles and Descriptions

YouTube hashtags are easy to overlook, because they sit quietly above the video title or at the bottom of the description. But they power a real discovery channel: tapping a hashtag opens a page of videos using that tag, and YouTube uses tags to understand what a video is about.

This guide explains where YouTube hashtags appear, how many to use, which ones help, and the rules that can get a video's hashtags removed.

Where YouTube hashtags appear

The first three hashtags in a video's description appear above the title as clickable links. Any hashtags in the title itself also appear there. Tags after the first three stay in the description, where viewers rarely look, so the first three are the ones that matter most.

Clicking a hashtag opens a page of videos that use it, sorted by relevance. That page is a discovery channel, especially for niche topics where viewers browse by tag. Shorts can use hashtags too, and they appear in the Shorts description.

How many hashtags to use on YouTube

YouTube allows up to 15 hashtags per video. Use 3 to 5: the three that will show above the title, plus one or two more in the description. More than 15 means YouTube ignores all of them, so staying well under the limit is important.

Choose the three visible tags carefully. They should describe the video's topic, its format, and its niche. A video about a beginner guitar lesson might use #guitarlesson, #beginnerguitar, and #learnguitar. The visible tags are also a promise to the viewer, so they must match the video.

YouTube's hashtag rules

YouTube removes hashtags that break its rules, and it can remove all hashtags from a video if any of them are problematic. Misleading tags that do not match the video, tags that promote hate or harassment, and tags that are sexually explicit are all removed. Hashtags with spaces or special characters do not work, because the tag ends at the first space.

YouTube also ignores hashtags that are just a string of words with no meaning. Keep tags short, relevant, and honest. If a tag does not describe the video, leave it out, even if it is popular, because a misleading tag can cost you the visible tags as well.

Do hashtags help YouTube SEO?

Hashtags help YouTube understand a video's topic, but they are a small ranking factor compared to the title, description, watch time, and click-through rate. Their main value is the hashtag page itself, where viewers browse by topic, and the visible tags above the title, which signal the topic instantly.

For small channels, niche hashtag pages are a genuine source of views, because the competition is lower than in search. For large channels, hashtags matter less, because their videos are already recommended widely. Either way, tags take thirty seconds to add, so they are worth doing well.

Key takeaways

  • Use 3 to 5 hashtags per video; the first three show above the title.
  • YouTube ignores all hashtags if you use more than 15, so stay well under the limit.
  • Keep tags honest and relevant: misleading tags can get all hashtags removed.
  • Niche hashtag pages are a real discovery channel for small channels.

Frequently asked questions

How many hashtags can I use on YouTube?

Up to 15 per video, but 3 to 5 is best. Only the first three in the description appear above the title.

Do YouTube hashtags help videos rank?

Slightly. They help YouTube understand the topic and they power hashtag pages, but title, description, watch time, and click-through rate matter far more.

Why did my hashtags disappear from my video?

YouTube removes hashtags that break its rules, such as misleading or inappropriate tags, and can remove all hashtags from a video if any of them are problematic.
