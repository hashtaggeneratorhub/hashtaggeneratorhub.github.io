Privacy Policy

HashtagGenerator does not collect personal information. Our tools process your input locally in your browser and the text you type is never sent to our servers.

We do not use accounts, and we do not sell or share any data. If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you.

This site may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics in the future to understand which pages are popular. Any such analytics would be aggregate only and would not identify individual visitors.

Third-party links on this site lead to other websites with their own privacy policies. Please review those policies before sharing information with them.
